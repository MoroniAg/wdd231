Maintenance Quick-Guides

Chain Lubrication

Clean off the old grime, apply a small drop to each link, and wipe away the excess. Simple, but vital.

Tire Pressure

Check your sidewall for the recommended PSI. Use a floor pump for accuracy and a hand pump for emergencies.

Bolt Check

Periodically check that your seat post, handlebars, and pedals are tight and secure.
