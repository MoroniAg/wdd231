Ride Safety Tips

Helmet Always

No exceptions. Your safety is our priority. We can provide loaner helmets for new members.

Follow the Lead

Our ride leaders are trained in basic first aid and city cycling laws. Stay with the pack.

Check Your Gear

Ensure your tires are pumped and brakes are working before the meeting time.
